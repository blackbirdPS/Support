Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my progress?

Navigate to the Progress section to view workout history, weekly activity, training volume, and personal records.

Does Kadio save workouts automatically?

Yes. Kadio automatically saves your workout progress while you train.

What should I do if the app crashes or freezes?

Please try the following:

  • Close and reopen the app
  • Update to the latest version of Kadio

If the issue continues, contact support and include:

  • Your device model
  • Your iOS or Android version
  • Your Kadio app version
  • A description of the issue
